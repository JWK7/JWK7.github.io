Bellman Meets Lyapunov

Unsupervised Reinforcement Learning via Mastering Chaos · ICLR 2027 (under review)

Forward CIP (F-CIP) is an RL-native formulation of Controllable Information Production, an intrinsic objective grounded in the sum of positive Lyapunov exponents and defined by the system's dynamics alone. Maximizing it alone yields unsupervised balancing and self-righting behaviors; paired with a simple forward-velocity reward it produces upright hopping and running gaits that otherwise require hand-engineered rewards.

  • Authors: Wooyoung Chung*, Tristan Shah*, Volodomyr Makarenko, Juan Wachs, Stas Tiomkin (*equal contribution)
  • Technologies: PPO, MuJoCo-MJX, JAX
