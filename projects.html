Bellman Meets Kolmogorov

Unsupervised Reinforcement Learning Through Mastering Chaos · AAAI 2027

Forward CIP (Controllable Information Production) is an intrinsic reward derived from Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy that is directly compatible with standard RL algorithms. Maximizing it alone yields unsupervised balancing and swing-up behaviors; paired with a minimal forward-velocity reward it produces upright hopping and running gaits that otherwise require hand-engineered rewards.

  • Authors: Tristan Shah*, Wooyoung Chung*, Volodomyr Makarenko, Juan Wachs, Stas Tiomkin
  • Technologies: PPO, MuJoCo-MJX, JAX
